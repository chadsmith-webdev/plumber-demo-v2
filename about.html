How we grew

Our Story: From a Single Truck to Fayetteville's First Call

Ozark Plumbing Co. was founded in 2012 by Mike Davis, a master plumber with a simple mission: to provide plumbing services that people could actually trust. After years of working for larger companies where upselling was prioritized over honest solutions, Mike bought a used van, painted a logo on the side, and started taking calls.

Word spread quickly. Homeowners appreciated the straightforward advice, the fair, upfront pricing, and the fact that Mike always cleaned up after himself. One truck became two, then a small team. Today, Ozark Plumbing Co. is one of Northwest Arkansas's most trusted plumbing companies, but our core values haven't changed a bit. We're still just a local team dedicated to fixing your plumbing problems right the first time.
