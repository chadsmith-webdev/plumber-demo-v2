Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you get to my home in Fayetteville?

For standard appointments, we offer same-day service Monday through Friday 7am–6pm and Saturday 8am–4pm. For plumbing emergencies — burst pipes, active flooding, no hot water — we offer 24/7 emergency dispatch. Most Fayetteville customers see a technician within 2–4 hours of calling.

Do you give upfront pricing before starting work?

Yes. Every Ozark Plumbing Co. technician provides a written quote before any work begins. We won't touch a pipe until you've approved the price. We believe in transparent, flat-rate pricing — no hourly surprises, no hidden fees added after the job.

Are you licensed and insured in Arkansas?

Yes. Ozark Plumbing Co. holds Arkansas Plumbing License #PB-2847 and carries full liability insurance and bonding. Our technicians are background-checked and trained to Arkansas state plumbing code standards. You can request proof of insurance at any time.

What areas do you serve near Fayetteville?

We serve the entire 25-mile radius around Fayetteville, AR, including Springdale, Rogers, Bentonville, Centerton, Farmington, Prairie Grove, Goshen, Elkins, and West Fork. Not sure if you're in our service area? Call (479) 555-0182 and we'll confirm in under a minute.

What should I do if I have a burst pipe right now?

First, shut off your home's main water supply valve (usually near the water meter or in a utility area). Then call Ozark Plumbing Co. at (479) 555-0182 — we answer 24/7. While you wait, open faucets to reduce remaining pressure, and move valuables away from standing water.

Do you repair water heaters or only replace them?

We do both. Our technicians diagnose whether your water heater can be repaired (heating element, thermostat, pressure valve issues) or needs full replacement. We service all major brands and install both traditional tank and tankless water heaters. Most water heater calls are resolved same-day.
