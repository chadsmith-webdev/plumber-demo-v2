Common Questions

Still on the Fence? Here’s What Every Fayetteville Homeowner Asks First.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency plumbing service?

Yes. We’re available around the clock, every day of the year. Call (479) 555-0182 and we’ll mobilize immediately — our average emergency arrival time in Fayetteville is 47 minutes.

How quickly can a plumber arrive?

For emergencies, our average response time is 47 minutes. For scheduled calls, we offer same-day slots Monday–Friday 7am–6pm and Saturday 8am–4pm. We give you a precise arrival window, not a 4-hour range.

Do you provide upfront pricing before starting work?

Always. We give you a flat-rate price before picking up a single tool. No hourly surprises, no hidden fees, no add-ons after the fact. If something unexpected comes up, we discuss it with you before proceeding.

What areas in Northwest Arkansas do you serve?

We serve Fayetteville, Springdale, Rogers, Bentonville, Lowell, Siloam Springs, and surrounding communities in NW Arkansas. Call (479) 555-0182 to confirm service availability at your address.

Are your plumbers licensed, bonded, and insured?

Yes. Ozark Plumbing Co. holds Arkansas Plumbing License #PB-2847. All technicians are fully licensed, bonded, and insured — protecting you and your home on every job.

Can you replace my water heater the same day I call?

In most cases, yes. Our trucks carry the most common tank and tankless water heater models. Call before noon and we can usually complete same-day installation so you’re never without hot water overnight.
